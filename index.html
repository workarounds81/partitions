What we do

Three things, done properly.

We don't pretend to be a full renovation outfit. We specialise — which is exactly why our work is cleaner and our pricing is sharper.

Partition walls

Stud-and-track drywall partitions built straight, plumb and quiet. Room dividers, study nooks, extra bedrooms, storerooms, office cellular spaces.

  • Metal stud or timber stud framing
  • Single or double-layer plasterboard
  • Acoustic and fire-rated build-ups
  • Moisture-resistant board for wet areas
  • Door openings, service voids, skim & sand ready for paint

Painting

Proper prep, proper coverage. We fill, sand and prime before a brush touches the wall — because that's the part that decides how the finish looks in two years.

  • Full-unit repainting and new-unit painting
  • Crack filling, skim coating and sealing
  • Nippon, Dulux, Jotun and other brands on request
  • Feature walls, accent colours, spray finishes
  • Furniture wrapped, floors covered, site cleaned daily

False ceilings & L-box

Plaster ceilings, box-ups and cornices that hide the aircon trunking, the piping and the ugly bits — with clean shadow lines and level surfaces.

  • Full false ceilings and partial box-ups
  • L-box for cove and downlight lighting
  • Cornice and shadow-line detailing
  • Aircon trunking and pipe concealment
  • Access hatches where you'll actually need them

Something not on this list? Ask anyway — if it's adjacent to what we do, we'll either quote it or point you to someone we trust.

How it works

Four steps. No runaround.

Two ways to start: send us your drawings if you have them, or let us come and measure. Either way, you get a fixed price in writing before anyone lifts a tool.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    WhatsApp us a photo, a floor plan, an ID drawing, or just a description of the room. Rough is fine — we're used to reading between the lines.

  2. 02

    Quote on drawings or on site

    If your drawings are clear, we can price straight off them. If not, we'll come down, measure up and check the existing conditions. Site visits are free and there's no obligation.

  3. 03

    Fixed price, agreed scope

    You get an itemised quote: what's included, what's not, how long it takes. If you want to trim the scope to hit a budget, we'll tell you honestly where the savings actually are.

  4. 04

    We build it and clean up

    Our own crew on site, protection down before we start, daily clear-up, and a walkthrough with you at the end. Anything not right gets put right.
